# Online-Store-Sales-Dashboard
Developed an interactive Online Store Sales Dashboard using Power BI to analyze sales performance, profit, order trends, and regional distribution. Created interactive visualizations and KPIs to identify business trends, evaluate profitability, and support data-driven decision-making.
## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze sales and profit performance.
- Track monthly sales and profit trends.
- Evaluate regional and geographical performance.
- Identify patterns in order and sales data.
- Compare business performance using KPIs and interactive charts.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and reporting
- **Power Query** – Data transformation and cleaning
- **DAX** – Calculations and measures
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, KPIs, maps, and treemaps

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales and Profit Analysis
Analyzes sales and profitability to understand overall business performance.

### 2. Monthly Trend Analysis
Visualizes changes in performance over time to identify trends and fluctuations.

### 3. Geographical Analysis
Uses map visualizations to explore business performance across geographical locations.

### 4. KPI Monitoring
Displays key performance indicators to help monitor business results.

### 5. Sales Performance Visualization
Uses charts and treemaps to compare performance across different business dimensions.

### 6. Interactive Reporting
Provides visual reports that help users explore patterns and compare business metrics.

## 💡 Business Benefits
- Improves visibility into sales and profitability.
- Helps identify trends and performance variations.
- Supports geographical performance analysis.
- Enables faster interpretation of business data.
- Helps businesses make informed decisions using data.
